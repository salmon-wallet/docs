## Start from the official website

Go to [salmonwallet.io](https://salmonwallet.io) and choose the platform offered there. Treat the official website as the source of truth for currently supported platforms and downloads.

::steps
### Check the address

Before entering a password or recovery phrase, confirm that the domain is exactly the one published by Salmon. Do not trust links sent through direct messages, advertisements, or unsolicited support replies.

### Verify an extension listing

If you install a browser extension, open the store listing from the official Salmon website. Check the extension name, publisher, permissions, identifier, and update history before installing it.

### Create or restore your wallet

Choose **Create** for a new wallet or **Restore** only when you intend to import an existing recovery phrase. Make sure nobody can view or record your screen.

### Secure your recovery phrase

Record the recovery phrase offline and verify the backup before depositing significant funds. Never store it in email, cloud notes, chat, screenshots, or online forms.

### Connect to Solana dApps

For a first transaction, use a small amount. Check the selected network, destination, asset, amount, and fee before approving.

::

## Before adding funds

- Confirm that you can unlock the wallet again.
- Confirm that your offline recovery backup is complete and legible.
- Check the receive address on the final confirmation screen, not only in copied text.
- Send a small test amount and verify receipt before transferring more.
- Keep the browser, operating system, and wallet installation up to date.

::caution
Never enter your recovery phrase to “verify” an account, claim an airdrop, receive support, or connect to a dApp.
::
