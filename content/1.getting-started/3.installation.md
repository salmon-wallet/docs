## Use an official source

Open [salmonwallet.io](https://salmonwallet.io) and follow the download link for your platform. The official website is the source of truth for current store listings and supported platforms.

Do not install Salmon from links in advertisements, direct messages, search-result promotions, or unsolicited support replies.

## Browser extension

Salmon supports Chrome and Firefox. The extension runs in the browser side panel rather than a small popup.

Before installing:

1. Open the extension store from the official Salmon website.
2. Check the name, publisher, extension identifier, requested permissions, and update history.
3. Confirm that the listing has not redirected you to another domain.

After installation, pin or open Salmon from the browser toolbar. Your browser may ask you to enable side-panel access.

## Android

The Android app is available as a Beta release. Open its store listing from the official Salmon website and confirm the application identifier and publisher before installing.

## iOS and web

The iOS app is not yet generally available. The standalone web wallet was retired on September 2, 2026. Do not enter your recovery phrase into a site claiming to be the old Salmon web wallet.

## Before adding funds

- Create or restore the wallet in private.
- Verify that you can lock and unlock it.
- Make an offline backup of the recovery phrase.
- Send a small test amount before transferring more.
- Keep your browser, operating system, and wallet installation updated.

::caution
Never enter your recovery phrase to verify an account, claim an airdrop, receive support, or connect to a dApp.
::
