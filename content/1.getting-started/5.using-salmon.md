## Receive assets

1. Select the intended account and network.
2. Choose **Receive**.
3. Copy the address or show its QR code.
4. Confirm that the sender is using the same blockchain and network.

Solana and Bitcoin addresses are not interchangeable. Mainnet and developer-network funds are also separate.

## Send assets

1. Select an asset and choose **Send**.
2. Enter or scan the destination address.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Review the destination, network, amount, and estimated fee.
5. Approve and wait for the submitted transaction to resolve.

Salmon tracks pending Solana transactions across navigation, locking, and application restarts. A submission can have an unknown outcome when connectivity is interrupted; check the transaction in the selected blockchain explorer before sending again.

For Bitcoin, the client builds and signs the transaction locally and broadcasts it through public relays. Spendable balance can be lower than total balance because the fee depends on the selected UTXOs and transaction size.

## Activity and explorers

Open **Activity** to inspect recent transactions. Choose the preferred explorer in Settings to open an independent public view of a transaction.

Developer networks use different explorer URLs. Always confirm the explorer indicates the expected cluster or network.

## Solana collectibles

Salmon can list, inspect, send, and burn Solana collectibles. Burning is irreversible. Review the selected mint and account before approving.

The wallet separates loading failures from an empty collection. If collectibles fail to load, retry rather than assuming the account owns none.

## Contacts and addresses

The address book stores named contacts. Domain resolution may be available for supported naming systems, but always compare the resolved address before approving a transfer.

## Developer networks

Developer mode exposes Solana devnet/testnet and Bitcoin testnet. Assets on these networks have no mainnet value. Switch back to mainnet before using production funds.
